What the agent has learned from your edits

When you edit drafts, the agent can propose lessons for that kind of email. A lesson only goes live after a check: past emails are re-drafted with and without it, and both are compared with what you actually sent. Every change can be rolled back in one click, and changing lessons resets that category's auto-send counter.
