Inbox Agent

Sorts customer emails and drafts replies from the Knowledge Brain. A person approves every draft until a kind of email earns trust.

Which emails can go out on their own

A kind of email earns auto-send after 20 drafts in a row are sent without any change. One edit, rejection or failed spot check resets it to zero. Refunds, complaints, quotes, project work, follow-ups and account access always need a person.

What the agent has learned from your edits

After a few edits to the same kind of email, the agent can propose lessons. A lesson goes live only after a check: past emails are re-drafted with and without it and compared with what you actually sent. Every change can be rolled back in one click.
