    Every step is measured in EVALS.md.

    3. Check the evidenceIf nothing relevant is found, it doesn't guess.
    4. Answer with sources, or escalateUnanswered questions open an expert task; the expert's reply is reused next time.

      Knowledge status

      Each source is re-checked on its own schedule (set in Sources); only changed pages are fetched again. "Run refresh now" checks every source.

      Refresh runs

      What each refresh fetched. Times are in your time zone; hover a time to see it in UTC.

      URL health

      A page that answers 404 and is missing from its sitemap moves to the Archived KB at once. A 404 on a page its sitemap still lists may be a passing fault, so it's counted check by check. A 301 is followed: the page is re-pointed to its new URL and re-indexed there. Nothing is deleted.

      Archived KB

      Pages taken out of normal answers: gone from their site, or archived after review. They keep their text and passages and can be searched here on their own. Restore brings a page back: it's fetched again at the next check.

      Duplicates and change history

      A page found twice is stored and searched once: the same address spelled differently, the same page in two sitemaps, or the same text at two URLs. Every change to a page is recorded.
