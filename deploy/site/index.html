Three AI services that do a consultant's slow work

Knowing the product, answering the inbox, and turning client meetings into a priced scope of work. Each service is built for a human in the loop, and every model choice is backed by an eval.

Knowledge Brain Task 1

Answers product questions from 1,537 help-center articles and 5,000+ screenshots, with sources. What it can't answer goes to an expert queue.

  • Click an example question: paraphrases, screenshot-only answers, an off-topic question.
  • Answer the escalated question in Expert queue, then ask it again.

Inbox Agent Task 2

Sorts customer emails and drafts replies grounded in the Knowledge Brain. A person approves every draft until a kind of email earns 20 clean approvals in a row.

  • Open a suggested email and click Draft a reply.
  • Try the prompt injection (E019) and the pasted password (E035).

Scope Builder Task 3

A team of agents turns meeting transcripts into requirements, a product check, scope, estimate and proposal, and compares follow-ups with the approved version.

  • Open the built demo project, or create one from a real meeting.
  • Look at Changes on the follow-up version, then approve.

How they connect

The Inbox Agent and Scope Builder use the Knowledge Brain only through its HTTP API, so product facts come from one place.
